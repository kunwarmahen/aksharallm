Vision — a picture, into a model that has never seen one

Audio needed a codec to turn a waveform into integers. An image needs nothing of the sort: it is already a grid of numbers, so cutting it into patches makes a sequence directly. What is left is a bridge — and the bridge is two matrices. Read chapter 21.

image3 × 64 × 64 → 8×8 patches64 of them → ViTbidirectional → projector2-layer MLP → the FROZEN

No vision checkpoint yet. Render the corpus and train the projector — minutes, and no download: 
.venv/bin/python -m aksharallm.vision corpus --out data/vision/shapes --images 8000
.venv/bin/python -m aksharallm.vision.train configs/vision-shapes.yaml

Did it get the count, the colour and the shape?

Three attributes, scored separately. That is the whole reason the corpus is synthetic: the caption is known exactly, so this is a score rather than an impression — and a model that names the colour and the shape while never counting is a specific failure that one accuracy would average away.
